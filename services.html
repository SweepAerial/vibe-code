Service 01

Aerial Mapping

High-resolution aerial surveys that produce detailed point clouds, orthomosaic maps, and elevation models your team can actually use. Using photogrammetric processing, we capture precise spatial data across your site and deliver it in the formats your software already supports.

Key Deliverables

  • Dense 3D point clouds (LAS/LAZ)
  • Textured mesh models (OBJ/FBX)
  • High-resolution orthomosaics (GeoTIFF)
  • Digital Elevation Models (DEM)
Example Use Case

A civil engineering firm needed an accurate base map of a 400-hectare greenfield site before design commenced. We completed the survey in two flight sessions, delivering a 2cm GSD orthomosaic and DEM with a fast turnaround — replacing a week-long traditional survey.

Service 02

Construction
Visualisations

Turning drone imagery into polished visuals your clients and stakeholders can actually engage with. From aerial stills and edited video through to interactive 3D models hosted online, we give project teams a clear picture of site conditions and construction progress without needing to set foot on site.

Key Deliverables

  • High-resolution aerial stills
  • Edited site flyover video
  • Interactive 3D models (hosted online via Sketchfab)
  • Before/after progress comparisons
Example Use Case

A developer needed a compelling visual for a mixed-use project's presales campaign. We captured aerial imagery of the site and surrounding area and produced an interactive 3D visualisation that let prospective buyers explore the location — delivered as a browser-based viewer, no specialist software required.

Service 03

Construction Progress
Monitoring

Scheduled aerial surveys timed to your construction programme. We provide a consistent, objective visual record of site progress that keeps project managers, engineers, and clients informed at every milestone and flags deviations before they become problems. Every survey follows the same flight path, so your progress video looks seamless from first slab to handover.

Key Deliverables

  • Timestamped orthomosaic comparisons
  • Annotated progress reports
  • Side-by-side timeline imagery
  • DSM and elevation change maps
  • Repeatable flyover video from consistent flight paths
Example Use Case

A tier-1 developer needed monthly aerial records for a major mixed-use development across 24 months. We scheduled fortnightly flights, delivering annotated orthomosaics and 3D comparisons to their stakeholders via our client portal — providing a clear, audit-ready project timeline.

Construction Progress Monitoring
Service 04

Stockpile Volume
Calculations

Replace labour-intensive manual surveys with rapid aerial volumetric analysis. We calculate stockpile quantities and earthworks progress from drone imagery, delivering reliable volume estimates in hours instead of days.

Key Deliverables

  • Volumetric analysis reports (m³/tonnes)
  • Individual stockpile breakdowns
  • Comparison reports between surveys
  • Orthomosaic and DSM outputs for your records
Example Use Case

A quarry operator needed monthly stockpile reconciliation across 14 separate material types. We automated their survey workflow with monthly drone flights, delivering tonnage figures with a fast turnaround after capture — saving the client over 3 days of manual survey work per month.
