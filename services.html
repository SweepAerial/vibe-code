Service 01

Drone Mapping

High-resolution photogrammetric surveys that produce centimetre-accurate point clouds, meshes, and orthomosaic maps. Our RTK-enabled drones capture precise spatial data across sites of any size — from small residential lots to large mining operations.

Key Deliverables

  • Dense 3D point clouds (LAS/LAZ)
  • Textured mesh models (OBJ/FBX)
  • High-resolution orthomosaics (GeoTIFF)
  • Digital Elevation Models (DEM)
  • Ground Control Point reports
Example Use Case

A civil engineering firm needed an accurate base map of a 400-hectare greenfield site before design commenced. We completed the survey in two flight sessions, delivering a 2cm GSD orthomosaic and DEM within 48 hours — replacing a week-long traditional survey.

Service 02

Construction
Visualisations

We produce photorealistic 3D visualisations and immersive site models from drone imagery. From detailed textured meshes to interactive 3D environments, our visualisations give project teams, clients, and stakeholders a clear picture of existing conditions and construction progress.

Key Deliverables

  • Textured 3D mesh models (OBJ/FBX/glTF)
  • Interactive 3D scene files
  • Web-embeddable 3D viewer links
  • Before/after comparison scenes
  • Stakeholder presentation packages
Example Use Case

A developer needed a compelling visual for a mixed-use project's presales campaign. We captured aerial imagery of the site and surrounding area and produced an interactive 3D visualisation that let prospective buyers explore the location — delivered as a browser-based viewer, no specialist software required.

Construction Visualisations
Service 03

Construction Progress
Monitoring

Scheduled aerial surveys timed to your construction programme. We provide a consistent, objective visual record of site progress that keeps project managers, engineers, and financiers informed at every milestone — and flags deviations before they become problems.

Key Deliverables

  • Timestamped orthomosaic comparisons
  • Progress reports with annotations
  • Side-by-side timeline imagery
  • 3D model sequences for stage comparison
  • Secure client portal delivery
Example Use Case

A tier-1 developer needed monthly aerial records for a major mixed-use development across 24 months. We scheduled fortnightly flights, delivering annotated orthomosaics and 3D comparisons to their stakeholders via our client portal — providing a clear, audit-ready project timeline.

Construction Progress Monitoring
Service 04

Stockpile Volume
Calculations

Replace labour-intensive manual surveys with rapid aerial volumetric analysis. We calculate cut/fill volumes, stockpile quantities, and earthworks progress with high accuracy — giving you reliable inventory figures in hours instead of days.

Key Deliverables

  • Volumetric analysis reports (m³/tonnes)
  • Cut and fill heat maps
  • Individual stockpile breakdowns
  • Comparison reports between surveys
  • CAD-compatible surface exports
Example Use Case

A quarry operator needed monthly stockpile reconciliation across 14 separate material types. We automated their survey workflow with monthly drone flights, delivering tonnage figures within 24 hours of capture — saving the client over 3 days of manual survey work per month.

Service 05

Customised
Reports

Every project ends with a clear, professional report tailored to your team's requirements. We don't deliver raw data and leave you to make sense of it — our reports include annotated imagery, measurement summaries, change analysis, and actionable observations formatted for your workflow.

Key Deliverables

  • Annotated aerial imagery with measurements
  • Progress summary reports (PDF)
  • Change detection comparisons
  • Executive summary packages
  • Secure portal delivery with version history
Example Use Case

An infrastructure owner needed monthly aerial inspection reports for a 40km pipeline corridor. We structured a reporting template around their compliance requirements, delivering timestamped annotated imagery, observation logs, and change summaries — giving their asset team a consistent, audit-ready record.
