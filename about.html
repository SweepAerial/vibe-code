About Us

Built on Precision.
Driven by Data.

Sweep Aerial Photography is a Brisbane-based aerial data business built for the construction industry. Founded by a CASA registered remote pilot, we specialise in delivering clear, actionable spatial data to project teams across South East Queensland.

Our Story

How We Work

We combine drone capture with photogrammetric processing to produce orthomosaic maps, elevation models, 3D site models, and progress reports your team can actually use. From flight planning and airspace approvals through to final deliverables, we handle every step of the workflow.

We work closely with a small number of construction clients on an ongoing basis, which means every job gets our full attention.

Our Mission

What Drives Us

Three core principles guide every project we take on, from first contact to final delivery.

Precise

We never compromise on data quality. Every flight is planned, executed, and processed with care to deliver consistent, reliable outputs your team can act on.

Fast

We understand that project timelines don't wait. Every job is processed and delivered within a week of capture, ready to use without any back and forth.

Organised

Your project data is delivered in clearly structured, named folders so your team can find what they need immediately. No hunting through disorganised files.
