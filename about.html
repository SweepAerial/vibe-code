Our Story

Built on Precision.
Driven by Data.

Sweep Aerial Photography was founded with a single belief: that the best decisions are made with the best data. We started as a small team of surveyors and drone operators frustrated by the gap between what aerial technology could deliver and what traditional workflows actually provided.

Today, we operate across construction, mining, infrastructure, and environmental sectors — delivering survey-grade aerial intelligence that project teams can act on immediately. From flight planning and CASA airspace approvals through to final GIS outputs and client portal delivery, we manage every step of the workflow.

We invest in the best hardware and processing software available, and we stay at the leading edge of techniques like Gaussian Splatting and neural radiance reconstruction — so our clients are always getting the most accurate, most useful data possible.

Our Mission

What Drives Us

Three core principles guide every project we take on, from first contact to final delivery.

Precision First

We never compromise on data quality. Every flight is planned, executed, and processed to meet the highest accuracy standards available with current technology.

Fast Delivery

We understand that project timelines don't wait. Our streamlined processing workflows mean you receive processed, ready-to-use deliverables within 48–72 hours of capture.

Secure Delivery

Your project data is sensitive. All deliverables are hosted on our encrypted client portal, accessible only to authorised users, and retained for your project's full lifecycle.
