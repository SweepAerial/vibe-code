Our Story

Built on Precision.
Driven by Data.

Sweep Aerial Photography was founded with a single belief: that the best decisions are made with the best data. We started as a small team of surveyors and drone operators frustrated by the gap between what aerial technology could deliver and what traditional workflows actually provided.

Today, we operate across construction, mining, infrastructure, and environmental sectors — delivering high-accuracy aerial intelligence that project teams can act on immediately. From flight planning and CASA airspace approvals through to final GIS outputs and client portal delivery, we manage every step of the workflow.

We invest in the best hardware and processing software available, and we stay at the leading edge of 3D reconstruction and visualisation techniques — so our clients are always getting the most accurate, most useful data possible.
