FAQ

Common Questions

What areas do you operate in?

We operate Australia-wide. Our team is based in [Your City] but we regularly travel for projects across all states and territories. Travel costs may apply for remote or regional locations — we'll include these in your quote.

Do you handle CASA airspace approvals?

Yes. We hold a CASA ReOC (Remotely Piloted Aircraft Operator's Certificate) and manage all airspace approvals, NOTAM submissions, and site permissions as part of our standard workflow. You don't need to handle any aviation compliance — we take care of it.

How accurate are your survey outputs?

With RTK-enabled drones and ground control points, we can achieve horizontal accuracy of 2–3cm and vertical accuracy of 3–5cm. For applications requiring higher accuracy (e.g., cadastral surveys), we recommend discussing your specific tolerances with us upfront.

What file formats do you deliver?

We deliver in the formats your team already uses. Common formats include GeoTIFF (orthomosaics), LAS/LAZ (point clouds), OBJ/FBX (meshes), SHP/GDB (GIS data), DXF/DWG (CAD), and KML/KMZ (Google Earth). Just tell us what platform you're working in and we'll match it.
